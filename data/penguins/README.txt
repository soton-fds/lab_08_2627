Palmer penguins field data
==========================

One CSV file per species per field season, named <species>_<year>.csv.

Columns:
  species            Adelie, Chinstrap or Gentoo
  island             Biscoe, Dream or Torgersen
  bill_length_mm     bill length (mm)
  bill_depth_mm      bill depth (mm)
  flipper_length_mm  flipper length (mm)
  body_mass_g        body mass (g)
  sex                female or male
  year               year of the field season

Missing values are recorded as NA.

Source: the palmerpenguins R package (Horst, Hill and Gorman, 2020),
from data collected by Dr Kristen Gorman at Palmer Station, Antarctica.
